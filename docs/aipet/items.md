# Items

## Soulpass

The **soulpass（灵魂物件）** is a magical thing that endows an animal with a **soul**, making it a **souled animal**, or a **pet** in the game. There are preset and custom soulpasses.

The soulpass carries the **spell** which outlines the soul. Once a soulpass is applied to an animal, the spell it carries causes the animal's **primordial spirit（原初灵）** to *collapse* into a **determinate soul**.

## Soulcut

The **soulcut** is a scissor-like item that removes the soul of a pet. The pet would return to its primordial representation and lose its memories as a pet.
