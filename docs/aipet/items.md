# Items

## Soulpass

The soulpass is a magical thing that endows an animal with a soul, making it a souled animal, or a pet in the game. There are preset and custom soulpasses.

The soulpass carries the **spell** which outlines the soul. Once a soulpass is applied to an animal, the spell it carries causes the animal's primordial spirit to collapse into a determinate soul.

## Soulcut

The **soulcut** is a scissor-like item that removes the soul of a pet. Once the pet's soul is cut, it returns to its primordial existence and loses its memories as a pet.
